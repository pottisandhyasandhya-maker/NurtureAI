## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an empathetic, futuristic bridge between advanced synthetic intelligence and human physical wellbeing. It rejects cold, clinical sterility in favor of a radiant, life-affirming aesthetic termed "Luminescent Vitality." The system balances precise medical utility with gentle emotional warmth, creating an ambient sense of security, optimism, and proactive support.

The visual style blends soft glassmorphism, ethereal light projection, and organic curvature. Translucent, frosted planes hover over diffused chromatic auroras, suggesting a responsive intelligence living just beneath the surface. It avoids aggressive skeuomorphism or stark industrial minimalism, leaning instead into pill-shaped tactility, gentle optical blooms, and luminous focal states designed to look stunning on both handheld personal displays and expansive stage presentations.

## Colors

The palette uses radiant, nature-infused light frequencies suspended over pristine porcelain surfaces. Primary cyan-teal (`#0EA5E9` through `#14B8A6`) conveys restorative clarity, breath, and fluid responsiveness. Secondary violet-indigo (`#6366F1`) introduces cerebral intelligence, twilight calm, and synthetic awareness. Tertiary rose-peach accents (`#F43F5E` softening into `#FB7185`) supply physical vitality, circadian alerts, and emotional reassurance.

Neutral scales are built from deep twilight slate (`#0F172A`) down to luminous pearl mist (`#F8FAFC`). Pure black is strictly avoided to preserve chromatic softness.

### Color Distribution & Roles
- **Canvas Base:** Soft warm mist (`#F8FAFC`) with low-contrast ambient aura overlays.
- **Glass Surfaces:** Pure white at 65%–85% alpha (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`), balanced against translucent borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)` on top-left light vectors, `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)` on bottom-right drop shadows).
- **Text & Contrast:** Text layers default to slate-900 (`#0F172A`) for primary hierarchy, ensuring strict WCAG AAA conformance against white-tinted glass. Secondary text sits at slate-500 (`#64748B`).
- **Luminescent Gradients:** Interactive AI focal points utilize three-point radial gradients blending `#0EA5E9` (40%), `#6366F1` (35%), and `#F43F5E` (25%).

## Typography

The type system balances the friendly, open aperture of Plus Jakarta Sans for displays and interface signposts with the structural legibility of Inter for continuous vitals reading, contextual reports, and narrative care dialogue.

Display typography utilizes generous negative letter-spacing and optical kerning to project polished contemporary craftsmanship. Body copy prioritizes vertical breathing space with a 1.6x average line-height ratio, mitigating visual fatigue during high-stress health reviews. Numerical telemetry (heart rate, sleep cycles, timeline schedules) uses tabular figures in Inter to prevent layout shifting across animated metric feeds.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an adaptive multi-tiered responsive grid. While primary engagement occurs via a focused mobile viewport (single-column fluid feed with persistent lower anchor zones), the architecture gracefully expands for clinical dashboards and stage expo presentations.

### Breakpoints & Reflow Rules
- **Mobile (0 – 639px):** 4-column fluid layout. Margins: `16px`. Gutters: `16px`. Full-width modular cards with unified vertical stack cadence (`space-md` between units). Navigation anchors to a suspended glass dock at the bottom screen edge.
- **Tablet (640px – 1023px):** 8-column layout. Margins: `32px`. Gutters: `24px`. AI companion sphere and biometrics split into an asymmetrical 3:5 master-detail landscape view.
- **Desktop / Expo Display (1024px+):** 12-column layout. Max container width capped at `1200px`. Margins: `48px`. Gutters: `32px`. Layout decouples into a 4-column persistent AI presence and daily summary rail alongside an 8-column interactive care telemetry canvas.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical-refraction glass optics and warm chromatic projection rather than heavy opaque shadows.

### Atmospheric Planes
1. **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Luminous matte backdrop (`#F8FAFC`) layered with slow-drifting, deeply blurred radial color meshes (`#0EA5E9` at 8% opacity, `#6366F1` at 6% opacity, `#F43F5E` at 4% opacity).
2. **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Modules):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)` background with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(180%)`. Rimmed with a directional 1px border (`border-t: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`, `border-b: rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.2)`). Base shadow: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.03)`.
3. **Level 2 (Floating Controls & Popovers):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`. Elevated shadow: `0 20px 30px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.06)`.
4. **Level 3 (Interactive AI Orb & Critical Care Alerts):** Self-illuminating elements. Active layers generate dynamic ambient blooms via dual-ring drop shadows: `0 0 40px rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.35), 0 0 80px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.20)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft, protective geometry dominated by `rounded-3xl` (24px to 32px radius) and continuous pill closures. Sharp vertices are eliminated to reinforce safety, ergonomics, and organic intimacy.

- **Cards & Primary Modules:** Enclosed within `rounded-3xl` (1.5rem to 2rem) contours, conveying handheld comfort and physical softness.
- **Pills & Tags:** Completely hemispherical (`rounded-full`), used for real-time states, status badges, and chip filters.
- **Action Controls & Inputs:** Pill-shaped or softened `rounded-2xl` profiles that mirror card curvature at smaller scales.

## Components

### 1. The Interactive AI Orb
The living core of the daily companion.
- **Form:** A fluid, multidimensional sphere with shifting internal gradients composed of cyan (`#0EA5E9`), violet (`#6366F1`), and warm rose (`#F43F5E`).
- **States:**
  - *Resting/Ambient:* Slow rotational breath animation (8s period); soft cyan-indigo glow (`blur-2xl`).
  - *Listening:* Scale expansion (`1.05x`), rose-peach luminescence increases, concentric ripples expand across the host card.
  - *Analyzing/Responding:* Rapid chromatic fluidity, subtle particle drift, and synchronized micro-glow pulses.

### 2. Elevated Glass Cards
- **Construction:** `rounded-3xl`, background in translucent frosted white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`), `backdrop-blur-md`.
- **Border:** Dual-weight optical gradient: top-left rim `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`, bottom-right `1px solid rgba(203, 213, 225, 0.3)`.
- **Content Spacing:** `p-6` on mobile, `p-8` on tablet/desktop.

### 3. Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Button:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`). Gradient fill (`linear-gradient(135deg, #0EA5E9, #6366F1)`). Crisp white label (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, SemiBold). Light bloom hover effect (`0 10px 20px -5px rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary / Glass Button:** Frosted white fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`), 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`), text in `#0F172A`. Active state features a soft inward depression (`scale-[0.98]`).
- **Care Trigger (Urgent/Affirmative):** Rose-peach accent (`#F43F5E`), pill geometry, accompanied by gentle outer radiant rings.

### 4. Status Chips & Pill Tags
- **Design:** Compact `rounded-full` enclosures with `px-3 py-1`.
- **Variations:**
  - *Vital Normal:* Soft teal backdrop (`rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.12)`), text `#0F766E`, leading 6px pulsing teal dot.
  - *Active AI Mode:* Translucent violet backdrop (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.12)`), text `#4338CA`.
  - *Alert / Medication Pending:* Soft rose backdrop (`rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.12)`), text `#BE123C`.

### 5. Input Fields
- **Design:** Pill-shaped or `rounded-2xl` containers. Background at `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`. Inset shadow: `inset 0 2px 4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Focus State:** 1.5px solid boundary transitioning to cyan-teal (`#0EA5E9`), surrounded by an outer 4px luminescent wash (`rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.15)`).

### 6. Health Metric Lists & Telemetry Items
- **Structure:** Frameless segmented rows resting inside an elevated master card.
- **Visuals:** Icon encapsulated in an organic squircle container with low-saturation chromatic fill; primary data rendered in bold Plus Jakarta Sans; auxiliary units in Inter tabular digits. Separators use translucent hair lines (`rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.15)`).
